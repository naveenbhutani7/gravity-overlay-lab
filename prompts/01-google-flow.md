# Prompt 1 — Google Flow

Create a short, realistic video of a single ripe red apple falling from a tree.
Use a steady, locked camera and natural daylight. Keep the apple clearly
visible against the background throughout the fall. Use one continuous shot:
no cuts, camera moves, text, labels, arrows, or other graphics. Leave some
clear space beside the apple so an educational overlay can be added later.

> This is a ready-to-use starter prompt based on the sample clip, not a
> transcript of the original prompt sent to Google Flow.
